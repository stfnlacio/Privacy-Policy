This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and handled when you play games published by [Stefan Lacio / GameByStefanLacio] ("we," "our," or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data associated with your gameplay. This policy applies to all games published by us across any platform (e.g., PC, mobile, consoles, web browsers).

1. Information We Collect
Depending on the platform you are using to play our games, data collection generally falls into two categories:

A. Data Collected Automatically by Platforms
When you download, install, or play our games through third-party platforms (such as Uptodown, APKPure, Itch.io or Google Play Store.), those platforms may automatically collect certain technical and usage data. This can include:
Device information (hardware model, operating system version)
General geographic region or country
Gameplay statistics, achievements, and playtime
Crash logs and diagnostic data
Note: We do not directly access, collect, or store your personal platform account credentials (such as passwords or financial/payment details). All transactions and account management are handled securely by the respective platform storefronts.

B. Data Collected Directly by Us
In-Game Data: Some of our games may save your progress, high scores, custom settings, and preferences locally on your device (using local storage, save files, or cloud saves provided by the hosting platform).
Analytics and Telemetry (If Applicable): Certain games may utilize lightweight analytics tools to help us understand gameplay performance, troubleshoot bugs, and improve user experience. This data is typically anonymized and aggregated. If a specific game includes third-party analytics or advertising networks, it will be explicitly noted in that game's store listing.

2. How We Use Information
We use the limited data available to us for the following purposes:
To maintain, operate, and improve our games.
To fix bugs, analyze crashes, and optimize game performance.
To save your game progress and preferences.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:
Platform Providers: With platform operators (like Steam or Google) strictly as necessary to provide platform features like cloud saves, leaderboards, or achievements.
Legal Obligations: If required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Children's Privacy
Our games are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us outside of standard platform collection, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

5. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect any data associated with our games. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time toTime. Any changes will be posted directly to this GitHub repository (README.md), and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.
